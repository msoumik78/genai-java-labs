# Compensation Bands

Salary bands are reviewed twice a year by People Operations. This document is
restricted: sharing band information outside People Operations and HR is a policy
violation.

## Engineering bands (USD base)

| Level | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Junior (E2) | 72,000 | 85,000 | 98,000 |
| Mid (E3) | 98,000 | 118,000 | 138,000 |
| Senior (E4) | 138,000 | 165,000 | 192,000 |
| Staff (E5) | 185,000 | 215,000 | 245,000 |

## Off-cycle adjustments

Market adjustments may be applied at any time for staff at or above E4, capped at
12% of base in a rolling 12-month window. Adjustments are approved by the
compensation committee, not by individual managers.

## Bonus targets

Individual contributor bonus target is 10%. Manager bonus target is 15%. Payout is
weighted 70% company performance, 30% individual rating.
