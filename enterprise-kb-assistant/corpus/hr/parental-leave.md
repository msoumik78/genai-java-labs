# Parental Leave Policy

All permanent employees are eligible for parental leave regardless of contract type.

## Eligibility

Employees become eligible after **12 months of continuous service**. Employees who join
mid-project are still covered from their first day for adoption leave.

## Duration

| Situation | Paid weeks | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Birth mother | 26 weeks | Includes recovery period |
| Second parent | 16 weeks | Must be taken within first year |
| Adoption | 16 weeks | From placement date |
| Shared leave | 8 weeks | Transferable between parents |

## Applying

Apply through the HR portal at least **8 weeks before the leave start date**. Late
applications are considered only in medical circumstances.

Managers must not refuse leave for employees who meet the eligibility rule; escalation
goes to the People Operations team.
