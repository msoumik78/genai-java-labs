# Expense Policy

This policy covers all business travel and related spending for permanent employees
and contractors.

## Daily limits

| City tier | Meals per day | Incidentals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 (London, NYC) | 75 USD | 40 USD |
| Tier 2 (Berlin, Austin) | 60 USD | 30 USD |
| Tier 3 (all other) | 45 USD | 25 USD |

## Approval thresholds

- Under 500 USD: no approval needed, receipt required
- 500 to 5,000 USD: direct manager approval
- Above 5,000 USD: finance director approval required before booking

## Submission deadline

Expenses must be submitted within **30 days of the transaction date**. Submissions
later than 60 days require written justification and are approved at Finance's
discretion.

Contractors follow the same limits but require a purchase order reference on every
claim.
