# Pump warranty

Northwind Field Service covers the Harbor series transfer pump for eighteen months after the install date, but only when the owner registers the serial number within fourteen days. Registration is done on the customer portal, not by mailing the paper card.

The impeller housing uses gasket ZX-4419. That gasket is a wear item. It is replaced at no charge during the first registered year, and it is not covered after month twelve even if the pump warranty is still open. A lookalike gasket, ZY-100, fits the older Coastal series and is never a warranty part. Installing ZY-100 on a Harbor pump voids the housing claim.

If the seal weeps after a freeze, the owner must photograph the ice line before thawing the unit. Claims without that photograph are declined. Labor for a warranty gasket swap is included when a Northwind technician does the work. Customer-supplied labor is reimbursed at forty dollars, not at the shop rate.
