# Technician credentials

Nobody operates the vapor recovery unit until card T-7 is current. The card is issued after the classroom day and a supervised first start. A general mechanical license does not replace T-7.

Card T-7 expires on the anniversary of issue. A lapse of even one day means the technician may ride along but may not touch the valves. Renewals are booked through the training desk, not through dispatch.

Helpers may carry hoses and log pressures. They may not sign the startup sheet. The startup sheet is signed by the T-7 holder who opened the vapor valves, and that signature is what the auditor checks.
