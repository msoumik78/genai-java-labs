# Parts returns

Sealed solvent kits cannot be sent back. The kit is considered used once the neck band is broken, and an unopened kit is still final sale because the solvent is batch-traced. Do not accept a solvent kit into the returns cage.

Open filter cartridges may be returned within thirty days if the element is dry and the plastic wrap is the original wrap. Write the work-order number on the bag. Cartridges that smell of fuel are scrap.

Credit is issued to the original payment method within ten business days. Store credit is offered only when the card that paid has expired. Freight on a refused solvent kit is billed to the shop that shipped it.
